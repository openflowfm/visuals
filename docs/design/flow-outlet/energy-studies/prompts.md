# Energy vessel studies

Generated using the built-in image-generation tool. Static visual explorations, not implemented behavior or renderer screenshots. Pulse frames represent successive moments; motion quality must be judged later in a prototype.

Design intent: wordless overview; pictures and restrained icons; material unity between node and breakouts; gentle activity inside stable shells; controls revealed locally by hover, focus, or tap; completed usable results rather than obligatory refinement.

## Study 1

Use case: ui-mockup. High-resolution landscape 3:2 visual exploration board for visual[flow], a creative node graph instrument. This is a static designer's exploration, not production UI. Premium restrained glossy smoked-glass digital instruments, energy and procedural pictures INSIDE vessels, subtle reflections, no cyberpunk gaming aesthetic or neon outlines. Dark charcoal backdrop, unusually generous spacing, crisp small editorial captions OUTSIDE UI specimens. Nodes themselves have NO WORDS at rest. Small pictogram sockets distinguish picture (framed image), numeric modulation (pulse), position (crosshair), not color alone. User wants pictures and taste, not technical parameter lists. Gloss is intentional, internal light is soft. Avoid old dashboard cards or presentation boxes around every specimen. All controls and breakouts must feel materially continuous with their parent vessel. All labels easy to read. This board should feel like a thoughtful industrial/UI design studio contact sheet, not an infographic. Title "01 / Vessels". Subtitle "Six ways to hold a live picture". Six large ISOLATED node objects arranged with plenty of negative space, 3 across by 2 down, no wires between specimens. Same magenta-cyan ripple checker image inside all six for comparison. Each object has one small attached inlet on left and a small bundled flow outlet right. No titles or words on the objects. Under each specimen OUTSIDE UI label and short sentence:
"A  Smoked lens" / "A thin dark rim around a luminous picture." Shape shallow rectangular lens, barely rounded corners, smoked glossy beveled glass.
"B  Soft capsule" / "A rounded vessel with a warm internal glow." Squircle, softly curved volume, not a ball.
"C  Edge reservoir" / "Light gathers near the connection." Dark thin glass panel, image reaches edge, small luminous chamber integrated into neck.
"D  Ceramic shell" / "A quiet frame around liquid color." Satin black sculpted ceramic surround, glossy glass contents, attached neck in same material.
"E  Clear chamber" / "The signal feels suspended inside." Semitransparent thick glass shallow rounded rectangular volume, restrained caustics.
"F  Tensioned membrane" / "The surface gently responds to the signal." Extremely thin organic rounded rectangle, slight inflated glass lens surface, tidy integrated sockets.
Keep these genuinely different shapes and materials, not six identical rectangles. NO heavy drop shadows, NO disconnected pills, NO large halos, NO literal laboratory tubes, no icons for delete or menus at rest. Low-amplitude motion suggested only by subtle internal gradients. No waveform, sliders, or extra text. Each object should be large enough to judge finish and silhouette.
